# World Lens

World Lens is a Chrome extension that helps readers detect bias and build a constructive perspective on global news.

Instead of reading a story from only one outlet, country, or language, World Lens lets users compare how the same event is covered across the world. It surfaces related articles from international sources, organizes them by country, and highlights how framing, emphasis, and narrative can shift depending on where the story is reported.

## Vision

Modern news consumption is fragmented. A single article can make an event feel obvious, one-sided, or complete, even when other regions are focusing on different causes, consequences, actors, or stakes.

World Lens is built around a simple idea:

> Better perspective comes from seeing how the same story looks through different lenses.

The goal is to help users notice bias, compare viewpoints, and form a more informed understanding of complex events.

## What World Lens Does

World Lens analyzes the article a user is currently reading and finds related coverage from other countries and sources.

It helps users see:

- which parts of the story different countries emphasize
- whether coverage is local, political, economic, cultural, or security-focused
- how headlines and framing differ across regions
- which sources are reporting similar facts with different narratives
- where a story is being heavily covered versus barely discussed

## Features

- Opens directly in Chrome’s side panel
- Extracts the current article’s headline, URL, and text
- Searches for related international coverage
- Groups results by country
- Shows article source, headline, country, and link
- Translates non-English headlines
- Summarizes regional framing and coverage angle
- Helps users compare narratives across countries
- Visualizes global coverage through an interactive world view

## Purpose

World Lens is for readers who want to move beyond passive news consumption.

It is designed for people who ask:

- What am I missing from this story?
- How might another country frame this differently?
- Is this article emphasizing one angle while ignoring another?
- What would a more balanced perspective look like?

By making global comparison easier, World Lens helps users approach news with more context, less tunnel vision, and a stronger ability to recognize bias.

## Goal

Open one article. Launch World Lens. See the world’s perspective.
